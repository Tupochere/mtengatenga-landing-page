Preserving Malawi's Hidden Heritage

The Mtengatenga Museum holds incredible stories within its artifacts, but much of this history remains hidden behind static display cases. Our project aims to change that.

By leveraging Augmented Reality (AR) and Artificial Intelligence, we are bringing these historical artifacts to life. We scan physical artifacts to unlock rich, interactive stories, detailed 3D models, and provide a personalized AI guide for every visitor.

This digital preservation not only enhances the museum experience but ensures that Malawi's cultural heritage is accessible and engaging for future generations.

Installing the APK, step by step

  1. 1

    Tap Android APK (Sideload) above. Your browser will warn that this type of file can harm your device — that warning appears for every APK downloaded outside the Play Store. Choose Download anyway.

  2. 2

    Open the downloaded file. Android will ask you to allow installs from this source. Go to Settings › Apps › Special access › Install unknown apps, pick the browser you downloaded with, and turn on Allow from this source.

  3. 3

    Play Protect may show “Unsafe app blocked”. This is because the build is not signed by a Play-registered developer, not because anything was detected in it. Tap More details › Install anyway to continue.

  4. 4

    Launch the app and grant camera access when prompted — the scanner cannot work without it. The app requests no other permissions.

  5. 5

    Unzip the QR Test Pack and print the codes, or just display them on a second screen. Point the app's scanner at one to load that artifact.

Common Questions

Is it free?

Yes, it's a student demo project and completely free to try.

Does it work on iOS?

Currently, the application is Android-only.

Can I use it at home?

Yes, you can download the QR Test Pack, print the codes, and scan them anywhere to experience the AR features.

Why does my phone warn me about the app?

Because it is installed directly rather than through the Play Store. Android shows that warning for any sideloaded app. See the install guide for what to expect.
